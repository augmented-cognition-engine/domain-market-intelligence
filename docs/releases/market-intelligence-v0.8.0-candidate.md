# Market Intelligence 0.8.0 candidate

## Release intent

Market Intelligence 0.8.0 aligns the root distribution with the already compiled 0.8.0 Domain Pack
and adds a separately packaged trusted Builder adapter for the bounded ACE v1 Market experience.

The root `ace-domain-market-intelligence` wheel remains inert JSON-only data. The optional
`ace-app-market-intelligence-builder` distribution contributes two distinct entry points:

- an authority-neutral planner that proposes the exact Pack, two reviewed OpenAI publications,
  activation requirements, and effects for user review; and
- a trusted executor that, only after Core authorization, uses Core-owned recorded admission,
  PREPARED derivation, first-Brief, source-health, and resource-plane ports.

The adapter also owns the bounded Market direction artifact: objective, audience, story
architecture, content hierarchy, approved cited claims, constraints, open questions, and required
assets derived from one exact Brief and user Decision. It is content-addressed, contains no rendered
design, and is wrapped as an effect-free prepared handoff for Core's existing AC5 gate. Exact
package-plus-destination authority is still resolved by Core immediately before delivery.

## Frozen bounded scenario

The candidate binds the July 9 and July 30, 2026 GPT-5.6 Terra pricing publications to the exact
`product:gpt-5-6-terra-input-tokens` subject. The declared `product_price_move` detector evaluates
the recorded USD 2.50 to USD 2.00 progression and routes a competitive price Signal to the declared
Market persona and cited Brief policy.

These materials are deterministic recorded fixtures. They do not prove a current network response
or current pricing.

## Compatibility

- Root Domain Pack: Python 3.12, `ace-core>=0.8.3,<1.1`.
- Trusted Builder adapter: Python 3.12, `ace-core>=0.9,<1.1`,
  `ace-domain-market-intelligence>=0.8,<1`.
- Historical public-product adapter: unchanged and independently versioned; it is not used to
  fabricate live acquisition in this scenario.

## Publication gate

This packet is candidate evidence, not a publication receipt. Publication requires compatible
public Core artifacts plus a clean public-artifact-only reproduction of reviewed plan, exact
approval, admission, source health, Shift/Signal, cited first Brief, restart, and later update.

## Explicit exclusions

This release does not claim a universal connector catalog, owned customer-data ingestion, live
website extraction, autonomous delivery, autonomous policy updates, rendered marketing artifacts,
or general customer-corpus relevance. Acknowledged delivery and outcome-return proof remain
coordinated Core/solution-bundle release gates rather than a second Market runtime.
